title: Crypto, Flights, and a one-card gallery
---

Two cards shipped, Crypto and Flights, and the homepage shows one card at a time.

## New

- [Crypto](/docs/crypto): A live crypto card with price, change, market cap, volume, and a price line with a 24H, 1W, 1M, 1Y, and All switch. The docs page shows six coins in each range.
- [Flights](/docs/flights): A live boarding pass shaped like a ticket, looked up from the flight number, and a travel assistant that books flights and checks you in with no chat box. The `variant` prop draws a world map on the theme's card color or paints the ticket in the airline's color, and the docs page shows both for 22 airlines.

## Updated

- [Gallery](/): The homepage shows one card at a time and moves to the next every 5 seconds. Dots show which card is up, and the gallery pauses while the pointer or focus is on it.
- **Playgrounds**: Recordings also save Crypto and Flights data, so their replays call no price or flight service.
- **Cards**: Quote, Crypto, and Weather share one glass frame. The cards have no drop shadow, and each card draws one edge instead of two or three stacked outlines.
