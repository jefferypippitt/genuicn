title: Quote, Weather, and a gallery homepage
---

The first two cards shipped, Quote and Weather, and the homepage became a gallery of the cards.

## New

- [Quote](/docs/quote): A live stock quote card with price, change, and a trend line with a 1D, 1W, 1M, and 1Y switch.
- [Weather](/docs/weather): A live weather card with the temperature, conditions, and the next six hours. The sky behind it is drawn for each of seven times of day and eight conditions, and the docs page shows all of them.
- [Gallery](/): The homepage is a grid of tiles for the cards. The Quote and Weather tiles show the real cards with data saved on October 15, and a shipped card's tile links to its docs page.

## Updated

- **Sidebar**: Only pages that exist are listed, starting with the Gallery.
- **Playgrounds**: Recordings save the data each card loaded, so a replay calls neither the model nor the quote and weather services. `pnpm record:data` refreshes that data without a model call.
- [Quote](/docs/quote): The `showAsOf` prop hides the "As of" time, for a card that shows saved data.
