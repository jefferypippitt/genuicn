- [Area Chart](/docs/area-chart): An area chart for a question with numbers over time, like world population since 1960. The card looks the numbers up on the web with Parallel and gives each subject its own tab.
- [NBA scoreboard](/docs/scoreboard): One game's score from ESPN, with both teams' logos and records, the clock, and the points in each quarter.
- [NBA standings](/docs/standings): The East or West regular-season table, with wins, losses, games behind, home and away records, the last ten games, and the streak.
- [NBA Cup](/docs/nba-cup): One NBA Cup group's table, with games played, wins, losses, and points for and against.
- [NBA player](/docs/player): A row per player with a photo, number, height, age, and points, assists, rebounds, and steals per game. Several players can share one table.
- [NBA win probability](/docs/win-probability): Both teams' chance of winning one game. Before tip-off it shows ESPN's prediction, and during the game it updates every 30 seconds.
- [Crypto](/docs/crypto): A coin's price, change, market cap, and volume, with a price line you can switch between 24H, 1W, 1M, 1Y, and All.
- [Flights](/docs/flights): A boarding pass looked up from the flight number. The ticket sits on a world map or takes the airline's color.
- [Quote](/docs/quote): A stock's price and change, with a trend line you can switch between 1D, 1W, 1M, and 1Y.
- [Weather](/docs/weather): The temperature, the conditions, and the next six hours. The sky behind the card is drawn for seven times of day and eight conditions.
