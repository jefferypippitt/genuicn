title: Five NBA cards, an NBA group in the sidebar, and a shared card frame
---

Five NBA cards shipped, the sidebar groups them under NBA, and the data cards now share one frame.

## New

- [NBA scoreboard](/docs/scoreboard): One game's score from ESPN with both teams' logos and records, the clock, and the points scored in each quarter. An NBA Finals game names its game number and shows the series under the score.
- [NBA standings](/docs/standings): A conference's regular-season table on Fluid Functionalism's Table, with wins, losses, games behind, home, away, and last-ten records, and the streak. The model sets `conference` to `east` or `west`.
- [NBA Cup](/docs/nba-cup): One NBA Cup group's table with games played, wins, losses, and points for and against. The data comes from the NBA's own stats API, which can block requests from cloud servers.
- [NBA player](/docs/player): A row per player with a photo, number, height, age, experience, and points, assists, rebounds, and steals per game. The card looks up the player's current team, and a comparison puts several players in one table.
- [NBA win probability](/docs/win-probability): Both teams' chance of winning one game, drawn on Fluid Functionalism's Slider in the team's color, with what a win pays back. Before tip-off it shows ESPN's prediction, and during the game it reads the live chance again every 30 seconds.

## Updated

- [Gallery](/): The homepage gallery adds the NBA scoreboard, showing Game 5 of the 2026 NBA Finals.
- **Sidebar**: The Sports section groups the NBA cards under one NBA row that opens and closes.
- **Cards**: Quote, Crypto, and Weather drop the glass frame. The data cards import their frame from `lib/card-frame.ts`, so Quote and Crypto now match the NBA cards in dark mode.
- **Chat**: The chat and the flights check-in assistant scroll in Fluid Functionalism's ScrollArea instead of the browser's own scrollbar.
- **Docs**: The tabs that show a card's variants no longer have a paragraph above them.
